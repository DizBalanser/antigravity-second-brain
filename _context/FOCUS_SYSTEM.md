# ⚡ The Focus Architecture

## 1. The Rule of 3
Every day has dozens of distractions, but only 1–3 truly move the needle.
- **Priority #1 (The Milestone):** The ONE thing that makes the day a win even if everything else fails.
- **Priority #2 (Secondary Push):** Important progress on an active project.
- **Priority #3 (Maintenance / Admin):** Key communication, paperwork, or essential chores.

## 2. Daily Rhythm (The 3 Checkpoints)
- **🌅 Morning Kickoff (08:30):** Define today's ONE Milestone and clear blockers.
- **⚡ Midday Pulse (13:00):** Check progress on Priority #1; adjust course if derailed.
- **🌙 Evening Shutdown (22:00):** Celebrate the day's win, log reflections, and prepare mental space for sleep.

## 3. P.O.O.L. & P.A.R.A. System
- **`00_Inbox/`:** Quick dump for all unsorted thoughts, ideas, voice notes.
- **`10_Projects/`:** Active endeavors with a defined deadline and outcome.
- **`20_Areas/`:** Ongoing responsibilities to maintain over time (Health, Finance, Career).
- **`30_Resources/`:** Reference materials, docs, and cheat sheets.
- **`_pool/todo.md`:** Single source of truth backlog.
